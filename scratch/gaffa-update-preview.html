What's New · 2 September 2026

Scouting Reports, Player Profiles, and Deadline Day

Scouting reports and a page for every player, rosters up to 22, €30m for every club, and auction length now set by market value.

Scouting reports

Every player now carries a written outlook from Futbolpedia: how he's used, how secure his place is, what to expect week to week, and where he is in his career. It's written from match data, minutes, set-piece duty, and transfer news. It doesn't read your league's points and it doesn't give bid advice.

Each player also carries tags you can filter on — quality, how nailed-on he is, dynasty value, and risks to watch.

Player pages

Every player has his own page. The left side is football: the scouting report, minutes and starts, goals against xG, assists against xA, set-piece order, and his xGI/90 percentile among players in his position. The right side is your league: points, PPG, rating, owner, and this week's fixture.

Switch seasons and both sides move together.

Players, three views

The Players tab replaces Stats and opens three views of the same pool.

  • Cards, the default. Each card leads with the first line of that player's scouting report. Filter by quality, minutes role, and Watch for — the first way to ask who's leaving the Premier League.
  • Table, the old stats table, now with a gameweek filter.
  • Explorer, a scatter plot on any two of eight stats. Opens on market value against PPG. Tap a dot for the player.

Roster and budget

Roster size increased from 20 to 22. Every club gets €30m.

There's a blockbuster on the auction board to spend it on.

Auction timing

Auction length now depends on the player's market value:

Market valueMinimum length
Under €25m12 hours
€25m – €50m24 hours
€50m – €80m48 hours
€80m and above72 hours

After the last bid, a lot ends on inactivity, and that window shortens as the auction ages: 6 hours on day one, 4 after two days, 2 after three, 1 after four. Any bid resets it, so you can't be sniped.

Nothing settles between 11pm and 8am — those lots move to midday. Exception: an uncontested weekend lot under €20m settles at 6:45am, before the early kickoff. Auctions over €50m now open at midday instead of whenever the overnight sync runs.

Also new

  • The Club Balance pill opens a dropdown with every club's balance.
  • First-season cup brackets seed at gameweek 7 instead of straight after the draft. Leagues with a previous season still seed from real standings.
  • League Home's market board is a table, and shows how many of your opponent's players are still to play.
  • The fixture strip is day tabs with club badges, on the correct gameweek.
  • Message an opponent from their card on League Home.
  • The chat widget opens from the top bar on every page.
  • Search ignores accents: "munoz" finds Muñoz, "odegaard" finds Ødegaard.
  • Players show the name you'd actually call them.
  • Gameweek switching on Matchups is instant.
  • Stats, Players, and My Club remember your view, sort, and filters.
  • New signings show N/A until their position and value sync, instead of a placeholder.

Notifications

  • Outbid notices say whether you were outbid directly or passed in the order, and everyone who bid before you is told.
  • Auctions closing soon count down.
  • The inbox folds repeated tags.
  • Push notifications name the player or deal.
  • Auction emails rewritten.

What you're looking at

The pop-up is what fires once for a major update — the same row that lights the bell, so dismissing either clears both. The changelog is the full entry at /updates.

Both are drawn at 390pt, an iPhone 14/15 width, with 44pt touch targets on every button.

Two decisions worth a look

The pop-up leads with three highlights, not the whole entry. A modal that has to be scrolled is a page wearing the wrong clothes, and the entry is one tap away.

The changelog puts auctions above "around the league" because it changes how you play this week, not just what you see.
